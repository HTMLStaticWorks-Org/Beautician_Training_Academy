How We Evaluate & Certify Our Students

Our two-tier examination system ensures every graduate masters both creative artistry and clinical safety protocols.

01 • Bridal, Makeup & Hair Couture Board

Live Model Portfolio & Timed Practical Exam

To earn our Master Artistry Certification, candidates complete a timed 3-hour bridal and editorial transformation before an external jury of senior makeup directors and fashion photographers.

  • Color-matching accuracy across fair, olive, and deep melanin skin tones.
  • Brush sanitization, station ergonomics & HD camera flash testing.
  • Submission of 5 high-resolution graduation lookbook plates.
Explore Makeup Diploma Tracks
02 • Clinical Cosmetology & Dermal Board

Clinical Logbook, Anatomy Viva & Safety Audit

Our Clinical Aesthetics Certification is modeled on international medical spa standards. Students are graded on skin consultation accuracy, contraindication screening, and safe operation of electrical and chemical modalities.

  • Written examination in dermatology basics, skin histology & pH chemistry.
  • 120+ signed practical log hours recorded inside the Student Portal.
  • Hospital-grade autoclave sterilization & post-peel care certification.
