Structured Excellence

How Your Academy Training Unfolds

From your first day in the theory lab to your final graduation shoot and international certification.

I.

Counseling & Batch Enrollment

Meet our academic dean to choose your diploma track, select morning, afternoon, or weekend batch timings, and receive your Student Portal login.

II.

Theory, Anatomy & Pro Kit Issue

Receive your professional vanity and dermal tool kit while mastering skin histology, color wheel chemistry, and clinical hygiene standards.

III.

Live Model Practical Logs

Complete 80 to 600 hours of supervised practice on real models and clinical clients, logging every graded assessment in your Student Dashboard.

IV.

Portfolio Shoot & Certification

Direct your own studio lookbook shoot with professional models, receive your QR-verified international diploma, and interview with partner salons.
